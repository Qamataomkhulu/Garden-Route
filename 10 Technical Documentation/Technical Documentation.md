# Technical Documentation

## Garden Route Integrated AgriHub — Infrastructure Reference

---

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the technical infrastructure underpinning each stage of the AgriHub's Seed-to-Shelf operating model: Land, Water, Energy, Grow, Harvest, Deliver, and Shelf. It is a planning-level reference; detailed engineering specifications should be developed by qualified consultants (hydrogeologist, electrical engineer, agronomist) prior to construction of each phase.

---

## 2. Land

**Scope:** site assessment, preparation, and access infrastructure.

- Land assessment and topographic survey
- Soil testing and classification per intended crop zones
- Fencing and site security
- Access roads and internal site circulation
- Drainage works to manage seasonal rainfall
- Irrigation layout planning, coordinated with the Water and Energy systems
- Expansion zoning reserved for Phase 2 and Phase 3 infrastructure

**Related SOP domain:** site preparation and land management (see SOP Register).

---

## 3. Water

**Scope:** water security infrastructure supporting both production and community distribution.

- Rainwater harvesting from roofed structures (tunnels, packhouse, office)
- Bulk water storage tanks sized to Phase targets (100,000L Year 1 rising to community-distribution scale by Year 5)
- Water purification system compliant with SANS 241 drinking water standards
- Future borehole, subject to hydrogeological feasibility study and Department of Water and Sanitation licensing
- Water quality testing and record-keeping schedule

**Related SOP domain:** water purification and distribution operations.

---

## 4. Energy

**Scope:** renewable energy infrastructure removing dependence on grid load shedding.

- Solar array sized by phase: 5kW (Year 1) scaling to 75kW (Year 5)
- Battery backup for continuity through cloud cover and overnight operation
- Solar-powered pumping for irrigation and water purification
- Future cold-storage power allocation as packhouse infrastructure comes online

**Related SOP domain:** solar system operation and maintenance.

---

## 5. Grow

**Scope:** cultivation infrastructure across open-field, protected, and nursery production.

- Open-field plots for seasonal vegetable production
- Protected cultivation tunnels/greenhouses for climate buffering and extended season
- Seedling nursery for both internal production and third-party seedling sales
- Drip irrigation, fed from the Water and Energy systems
- Future hydroponic production line (Phase 3)

**Related SOP domain:** crop planning, planting, and cultivation.

---

## 6. Harvest

**Scope:** infrastructure and process for bringing produce off the land at peak quality.

- Harvest crews, tools, and containers scheduled by crop maturity
- Sorting and grading area adjacent to production zones
- Quality control checkpoints prior to packhouse handoff

**Related SOP domain:** harvest and post-harvest handling.

---

## 7. Deliver

**Scope:** logistics infrastructure connecting production to customers.

- Packhouse for sorting, packaging, and order aggregation
- Cold storage to protect quality and extend the selling window
- Delivery fleet, scaled from Phase 1 through Phase 2 fleet expansion
- Water delivery logistics, run in parallel with produce delivery routes where feasible

**Related SOP domain:** packing, cold-chain management, and delivery routing.

---

## 8. Shelf

**Scope:** the customer-facing systems that convert delivered product into recurring revenue.

- Household subscription management
- Institutional supply agreements (schools, restaurants, retail)
- Customer feedback and quality loop feeding back into the Grow and Harvest stages

**Related SOP domain:** customer onboarding and account management.

---

## 9. Cross-Cutting Considerations

- **Licensing and approvals:** water use authorisation, food safety compliance, and (for future hydroponic/hemp expansion) any additional regulatory approvals should be tracked against the phased capital plan, since licensing lead times can be a critical-path item independent of construction time.
- **Maintenance:** repairs and maintenance across water, solar, and irrigation infrastructure should be budgeted as an ongoing operating cost (see the Financial Model's Opex schedule), not treated as a one-time capital item.
- **Site-specific validation:** all figures in this document are planning-level. Final specifications should be confirmed through site-level soil, water, and climate studies before construction of each phase.
