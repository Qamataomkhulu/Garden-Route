# ESG & Impact

## Garden Route Integrated AgriHub — Nacirfa Dream Group

---

## 1. Impact Thesis

The AgriHub is designed so that financial performance and social/environmental impact move together rather than in tension. Every core revenue line — vegetable sales, water sales, seedling sales — is also a direct measure of community impact: tonnes of food produced, litres of clean water distributed, households served.

---

## 2. Environmental

- **Renewable energy:** solar-powered irrigation and purification remove dependence on grid electricity and associated carbon intensity, scaling from 5kW (Year 1) to 75kW (Year 5) installed capacity.
- **Water stewardship:** diversified water sourcing (rainwater harvesting, storage, purification, future borehole subject to licensing) reduces pressure on strained municipal supply during an active regional water crisis.
- **Climate-resilient production:** protected cultivation and diversified crop planning reduce exposure to weather volatility relative to open-field-only farming.
- **Future carbon/sustainability participation:** the long-term model includes potential participation in carbon and sustainability project frameworks as the AgriHub's renewable energy and land-use profile matures.

## 3. Social

- **Food security:** targeting 100+ tonnes of vegetables produced annually by Year 5, distributed through subscription boxes, institutional supply, and direct sales.
- **Water access:** targeting 5,000,000 litres of clean water distributed annually by Year 5 to surrounding households and community points.
- **Employment:** targeting 20–30 direct jobs by Year 5, growing from 5 in Year 1, with a payroll structure benchmarked against the national minimum wage sectoral determination.
- **Youth and skills development:** agriculture, water systems, and renewable energy operations create a hands-on training pipeline in sectors that are difficult to automate or offshore, supporting local skills development.
- **Household reach:** targeting 300+ subscribing households by Year 5, up from 30 in Year 1.

## 4. Governance

- Private company (Pty Ltd) structure under standard South African corporate governance.
- Documented SOPs across health & safety and food safety, prioritised ahead of first commercial harvest (see SOP Register).
- Transparent, phased capital deployment tied to specific infrastructure and KPI milestones (see Funding Strategy), designed to give funders clear line-of-sight from capital to outcome.

## 5. Impact Measurement Framework

| Impact Area | Metric | Year 1 Target | Year 5 Target |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Food security | Vegetables produced (tonnes) | 15 | 100+ |
| Water access | Water distributed (litres) | 100,000 | 5,000,000 |
| Renewable energy | Installed solar capacity | 5 kW | 75 kW |
| Employment | Direct jobs | 5 | 20–30 |
| Community reach | Households served | 30 | 300+ |

These metrics are drawn directly from the project's Financial Model and are intended to be reported against annually as actual performance is measured.

## 6. Alignment with Broader Frameworks

The AgriHub's impact areas — food security, clean water, decent work, and climate action — align directly with UN Sustainable Development Goals 2 (Zero Hunger), 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), and 13 (Climate Action). This alignment is offered as a reference point for impact investors and DFIs with SDG-linked mandates, not as a claim of formal SDG certification.
