# SOP Register

## Garden Route Integrated AgriHub — Standard Operating Procedures

---

## Purpose

This register indexes the standard operating procedures (SOPs) required to run the AgriHub safely, consistently, and to the quality standard expected by institutional and household customers. Individual SOP documents should be developed in full detail by the operations team as each stage of infrastructure comes online; this register defines the required scope and ownership for each.

---

## SOP Domains

| Domain | Covers | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Site preparation & land management | Land assessment, fencing, access, drainage | Land |
| Water purification & distribution | Purification operation, SANS 241 testing, storage management, delivery scheduling | Water |
| Solar system operation & maintenance | Panel/battery maintenance, load monitoring, fault response | Energy |
| Crop planning & cultivation | Planting schedules, crop rotation, pest & disease management | Grow |
| Nursery & seedling production | Propagation, seedling care, sales handling | Grow |
| Harvest & post-harvest handling | Harvest scheduling, sorting, grading, quality control | Harvest |
| Packing & cold-chain management | Pack-house procedures, cold storage protocols, packaging standards | Deliver |
| Delivery & route logistics | Route planning, vehicle checks, delivery confirmation | Deliver |
| Customer onboarding & account management | Subscription sign-up, institutional contract onboarding, billing | Shelf |
| Health & safety | Site safety induction, equipment handling, incident reporting | Cross-cutting |
| Food safety & hygiene | Handling standards, traceability, recall procedure | Cross-cutting |

## Governance

- Each SOP should have a named owner (role, not individual), a review date, and a version number once developed.
- Health & safety and food safety SOPs should be prioritised ahead of first commercial harvest, given institutional buyers (schools, government feeding schemes) typically require documented food safety compliance before onboarding as a supplier.
- SOPs should be reviewed at minimum annually, or immediately following any safety incident or quality complaint.
