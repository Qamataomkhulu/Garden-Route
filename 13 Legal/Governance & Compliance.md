# Governance & Compliance

## Garden Route Integrated AgriHub — Nacirfa Dream Group

---

## 1. Company Structure

Nacirfa Dream Group operates as a private company (Pty Ltd) incorporated in South Africa. As a private company, it is subject to standard South African corporate governance requirements under the Companies Act, and to the standard corporate tax rate, currently 27%, as reflected in the Financial Model.

## 2. Regulatory Considerations by Domain

### Water Use
Borehole drilling and abstraction, and any bulk water purification-for-resale activity, fall under South Africa's water use authorisation framework administered by the Department of Water and Sanitation. Water use for productive agricultural purposes and for resale to third parties typically requires registration and, above certain abstraction thresholds, a formal water use licence. Licensing lead times should be treated as a critical-path planning item.

### Food Safety
Fresh produce sold to households, schools, restaurants, and retail requires compliance with South African food safety regulations. Institutional buyers — particularly schools and government feeding schemes — may require additional certification such as GlobalG.A.P. or an equivalent good agricultural practice standard before onboarding as a supplier.

### Water Purification for Public Consumption
Water intended for public or bulk resale consumption must meet SANS 241 drinking water quality requirements, with regular testing and record-keeping.

### Future Hemp Operations
Any future industrial hemp cultivation or processing referenced in the long-term business model is explicitly licence-contingent and is not included in current revenue projections. South Africa's regulatory framework for hemp and cannabis cultivation continues to evolve; any future hemp division should be preceded by a dedicated legal and regulatory review at the time of implementation.

## 3. Employment & Labour

Agricultural employers are subject to the sectoral determination governing farm worker minimum wages. Payroll assumptions in the Financial Model are benchmarked against the current national minimum wage sectoral determination. As the AgriHub scales toward its 20–30 job target, formal employment contracts, health & safety compliance, and — where applicable — B-BBEE compliance documentation should be established ahead of institutional and DFI funding requirements, as most development finance institutions and larger corporate buyers require this documentation as a condition of engagement.

## 4. Environmental Compliance

Depending on final scale and site characteristics, activities such as borehole drilling, land clearing, and bulk water abstraction may trigger environmental authorisation requirements under South Africa's environmental impact assessment framework. This should be confirmed at site-selection stage through a qualified environmental consultant.

## 5. Governance Practices

- Maintain a clear separation between company funds and personal/founder funds from inception.
- Establish a documented decision-making structure (even at small scale) covering capital expenditure approval, hiring, and supplier contracts.
- Maintain audited or independently reviewed financial statements ahead of any DFI or institutional funding round, as this is a standard due diligence requirement.
- Maintain the SOP Register (health & safety, food safety in particular) as a living governance tool, not a one-time document.

## 6. Risk Disclosure

This document is a planning-level summary, not legal advice. Nacirfa Dream Group should engage qualified South African legal counsel to confirm current requirements across water use licensing, food safety certification, environmental authorisation, labour law, and company law before financial close on any funding phase, and before any future hemp-related expansion.
