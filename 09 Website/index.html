THE OPERATING ECOSYSTEM

One hub. Multiple systems. One resilient value chain.

Water supports production. Solar supports water and irrigation. Production feeds distribution. Distribution creates recurring demand. Food security, water resilience, energy resilience and economic opportunity reinforce one another across a single operating footprint.

We are not building only a farm.

The AgriHub is designed as infrastructure for a local food economy — combining production, water, energy, skills and distribution so resilience is built into the operating model.

CLIMATE RESILIENCE

Designed around Eastern Cape realities.

The model prioritises water storage, efficient irrigation, protected cultivation, renewable energy and local distribution. Final site selection, crop mix and infrastructure specifications should be confirmed through site-level climate, soil, water and hydrogeological studies.
