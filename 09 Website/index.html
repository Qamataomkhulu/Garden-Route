THE WHY

We don’t see food, water and energy as separate problems.

We see one connected system. Food production depends on reliable water. Water infrastructure depends on energy. Energy affects operating costs. Distribution determines whether production reaches a real customer. The AgriHub is designed to fix those connections together — creating a diversified agribusiness with commercial revenue and measurable environmental and social value.

INVESTOR FAQ

Questions a first meeting should answer.

What is the core investment thesis?

The AgriHub integrates food production, water resilience, renewable energy and distribution into a diversified operating model rather than relying on a single agricultural revenue stream.

How is capital deployed?

The current model structures R12.50M across three milestone-led phases: R4.85M Foundation, R4.50M Commercial Scale-Up and R3.15M Regional Hub Maturity.

What makes the model resilient?

Multiple revenue streams, protected agriculture, water storage and purification, solar-supported operations, local distribution and phased expansion are designed to reduce dependence on any one operating layer.

Are the financial figures audited or guaranteed?

No. The website presents illustrative project targets from the current model. Investors should review the underlying documentation and conduct independent commercial, technical and legal due diligence.

How can a strategic partner engage?

Use the investor interest form above to indicate whether the enquiry concerns investment, DFI funding, supply, distribution, technology, agriculture or community programmes.
