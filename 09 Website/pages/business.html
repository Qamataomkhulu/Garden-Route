The story behind the numbers.

The AgriHub is designed as a connected commercial ecosystem: land enables production, water and energy increase resilience, production creates inventory, and distribution converts capacity into recurring market access.

Food securityWater resilienceEnergy resilienceEconomic opportunity
THE COMMERCIAL LOGIC

Not simply a farm. A system of linked revenue layers.

The model combines agricultural production with supporting infrastructure and services so that one operating layer can strengthen another.

01

Food production

Vegetables, herbs and seedlings create the primary productive base across protected and open-field systems.

02

Infrastructure resilience

Water storage, purification, rainwater harvesting and solar-supported pumping reduce operational exposure.

03

Market access

Households, institutions, wholesale channels and future regional distribution convert productive capacity into revenue.

WHY THE LAYERS CONNECT

Every operating layer supports the next.

Water infrastructure supports agriculture. Solar supports pumping and critical operations. Production creates inventory. Distribution creates market access. Seedling propagation can serve the AgriHub and partner growers. Training and technical services can create additional commercial pathways.

The result is a diversified operating model rather than a single-product farm dependent on one crop, one customer or one utility.

YEAR 5 TARGET

R36.22M projected revenue

The target is supported by a diversified mix including vegetables, water services, wholesale produce, greenhouses, seedlings, consulting and training, renewable energy services and future hemp operations.
