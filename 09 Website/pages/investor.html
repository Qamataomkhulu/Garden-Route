INVESTOR FAQ

Questions a first meeting should answer.

What is the core investment thesis?

The AgriHub integrates food production, water resilience, renewable energy and distribution into a diversified operating model rather than relying on a single agricultural revenue stream.

How is capital deployed?

The current model structures R12.50M across three milestone-led phases: R4.85M Foundation, R4.50M Commercial Scale-Up and R3.15M Regional Hub Maturity.

Are the financial figures guaranteed?

No. They are illustrative planning outputs from the current project model and require independent commercial, technical and legal due diligence.

How can a strategic partner engage?

Use the investor interest form above to indicate whether the enquiry concerns investment, DFI funding, supply, distribution, technology, agriculture or community programmes.
