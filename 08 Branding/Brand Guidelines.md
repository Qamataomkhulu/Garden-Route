# Brand Guidelines

## Garden Route Integrated AgriHub — Nacirfa Dream Group

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

Garden Route Integrated AgriHub presents as a luxury-minimal, institutional brand — closer to a fine estate or a considered infrastructure fund than a conventional farm brand. The visual language should always read as elegant and restrained rather than decorative or agricultural-rustic.

## 2. Logo

- **Primary logo:** `Images/GR.png` — used in the website header and as the primary mark across documents and presentations.
- **Secondary/reversed mark:** `Images/GRnb.png` — used on dark backgrounds (footers, dark section bands) where the primary mark's contrast is insufficient.
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the mark itself. Do not stretch, recolour, or apply effects to either logo file.

## 3. Colour Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | `#123B2A` | Primary brand colour — headers, CTAs, key text |
| Deep Forest | `#092117` | Dark sections, footer, high-contrast backgrounds |
| Harvest Gold | `#C7A34B` | Accent — eyebrows, highlights, icons, borders |
| Light Gold | `#DEC66D` | Secondary accent, hover states, chart gradients |
| Cream | `#F4F0E5` | Section backgrounds, pills, soft contrast blocks |
| Paper | `#FBFAF6` | Primary background, card surfaces |
| Ink | `#17251E` | Primary body text |
| Muted Sage | `#64736A` | Secondary/supporting text |
| Leaf Green | `#4A8C5C` | Highlight accents, illustration elements |
| Sky Blue | `#5B8C9E` | Water-related iconography and illustration |
| Earth Brown | `#8B7355` | Land/earth-related accents, used sparingly |

Gold is an accent colour, never a dominant fill. Forest Green and Deep Forest carry the brand's visual weight.

## 4. Typography

- **Headings:** Playfair Display (600/700 weight) — used for all `h1`–`h3` elements. Serif, editorial, confident.
- **Body & UI:** DM Sans (400/500/600/700 weight) — used for body copy, navigation, buttons, and data labels. Clean and highly legible at small sizes for dashboards and KPI cards.
- Avoid introducing a third typeface. Weight and size variation within these two families is sufficient for all hierarchy needs.

## 5. Tone of Voice

- Confident, precise, and evidence-led — figures and claims should be specific rather than promotional.
- No emojis in any brand-facing material (website, documents, pitch materials).
- Prefer plain, declarative sentences over hyperbole. "100+ tonnes annually by Year 5" rather than "massive harvests."

## 6. Iconography & Illustration

- Illustration style is flat, geometric, and restrained — solid shapes and simple line work rather than photographic or highly detailed icons (see the hero landscape illustration on the website for the reference style).
- Category colour-coding: green tones for land/production, blue tones for water, gold for energy/solar.

## 7. Motion & Animation

- Animation should be subtle and purposeful: fade-up on scroll entry, gentle hover lift on cards, animated count-up for statistics, animated fill for bars and progress indicators.
- Avoid decorative or attention-seeking motion. Every animation should reinforce information (a number counting up to its real value) rather than exist purely for visual flourish.

## 8. Application

These guidelines apply across the website (`09 Website/`), investor documents, the pitch deck, and any press or partner-facing material. When in doubt, default to more restraint rather than more ornamentation — the brand's credibility depends on reading as institutional-grade rather than promotional.
